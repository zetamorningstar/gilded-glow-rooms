# Nil Mobilya Furniture Website

## Experience
- Build the home page as a premium, mobile-first furniture showroom in a dark charcoal and warm gold palette.
- Lead with “Nil Mobilya” and concise Turkish copy positioning the company as modern, refined, and high quality.
- Use an editorial layout with a strong full-width furniture image, restrained navigation, and a visible glimpse of the collection below.

## Content
- Add a curated collection area featuring three furniture categories.
- Include a concise craftsmanship statement and clear contact/collection actions.
- Keep all copy focused and realistic without inventing addresses, hours, or phone numbers.

## Motion and Interaction
- Add subtle pointer-following depth, image parallax, smooth reveal transitions, and tactile button/card feedback.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and ensure touch interactions remain natural on mobile.

## Technical Details
- Establish semantic dark/gold design tokens and typography in the global design system.
- Generate and bundle original furniture imagery rather than using remote placeholders.
- Add route-specific page metadata and verify the result at mobile and desktop sizes.
